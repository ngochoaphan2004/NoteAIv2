## 🗂️ Danh Mục Từ Vựng Học Thuật Cốt Lõi (20 Từ Mục Tiêu)

| Từ Vựng (Front) | Từ Loại | Phiên Âm (IPA) | Ý Nghĩa Tiếng Việt (Back) | Ví Dụ Ngữ Cảnh Trong Đề Thi | Collocations & Synonyms |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **pestilence** | `noun` | `/ˈpes.tɪ.ləns/` | Bệnh dịch hạch, dịch bệnh chết người | _After a pestilence that killed half the population in Milan, Leonardo turned to urban design._ | deadly pestilence, plague |
| **unconventional** | `adj` | `/ˌʌn.kənˈven.ʃən.əl/` | Khác thường, phá cách, không theo lối mòn | _His architectural designs were considered unconventional at the time._ | unconventional method, innovative |
| **sustainable** | `adj` | `/səˈsteɪ.nə.bəl/` | Bền vững, có thể duy trì lâu dài | _Unsustainable urban models are a key cause of global climate change._ | sustainable development, eco-friendly |
| **modernist** | `adj/noun` | `/ˈmɒd.ən.ɪst/` | Thuộc trường phái hiện đại | _The idea wasn't fully realized until the birth of the Modernist movement._ | modernist movement, architecture |
| **hydraulic** | `adj` | `/haɪˈdrɒl.ɪk/` | Thủy lực, liên quan đến sức nước | _Leonardo designed extensive hydraulic plants to create artificial canals._ | hydraulic system, power |
| **canal** | `noun` | `/kəˈnæl/` | Kênh đào nhân tạo | _The canals were supposed to make it easier for commercial boats to navigate inland._ | artificial canal, water channel |
| **navigate** | `verb` | `/ˈnæv.ɪ.ɡeɪt/` | Định hướng, di chuyển trên đường thủy/bộ | _Regulated by basins, the waterways helped boats navigate safely._ | navigate inland, navigate through |
| **adjacent** | `adj` | `/əˈdʒeɪ.sənt/` | Kế bên, liền kề | _The width of the streets ought to match the average height of adjacent houses._ | adjacent buildings, neighboring |
| **eruption** | `noun` | `/ɪˈrʌp.ʃən/` | Sự phun trào (núi lửa) | _The Laki volcanic eruption of 1783 caused widespread agricultural devastation._ | volcanic eruption, ash cloud |
| **devastation** | `noun` | `/ˌdev.əˈsteɪ.ʃən/` | Sự tàn phá, hủy diệt nặng nề | _The natural disaster brought immense economic and ecological devastation._ | cause devastation, disaster |
| **bracket** | `noun` | `/ˈbræk.ɪt/` | Khung phân loại, nhóm (thu nhập, tuổi) | _Households in the highest income bracket witnessed steady growth._ | income bracket, tax bracket |
| **disposable** | `adj` | `/dɪˈspəʊ.zə.bəl/` | Khả dụng (sau thuế), dùng một lần | _Rising disposable income contributed to increased consumer spending._ | disposable income, disposable products |
| **proportional** | `adj` | `/prəˈpɔː.ʃən.əl/` | Tương xứng, tỷ lệ thuận | _The expenditure was roughly proportional to household revenue._ | directly proportional, relative |
| **exponential** | `adj` | `/ˌek.spəˈnen.ʃəl/` | Theo cấp số nhân, tăng cực nhanh | _The tech sector experienced exponential expansion throughout the decade._ | exponential growth, surge |
| **counterpart** | `noun` | `/ˈkaʊn.tə.pɑːt/` | Đối tác, đối tượng tương đương | _Lower-income groups lagged behind their high-earning counterparts._ | international counterparts, peer |
| **rigorous** | `adj` | `/ˈrɪɡ.ər.əs/` | Nghiêm ngặt, chặt chẽ, chuẩn xác | _Urbanism emerged as a rigorous academic discipline during the Renaissance._ | rigorous testing, methodology |
| **specimen** | `noun` | `/ˈspes.ə.mɪn/` | Mẫu vật, tiêu bản khảo cứu | _Botanists collected numerous rare plant specimens from the rainforest._ | fossil specimen, lab sample |
| **scarcity** | `noun` | `/ˈskeə.sə.ti/` | Sự khan hiếm nguồn lực | _Severe water scarcity forced the council to introduce strict rationing._ | resource scarcity, acute shortage |
| **infrastructure** | `noun` | `/ˈɪn.frəˌstrʌk.tʃər/` | Cơ sở hạ tầng | _Modern cities require sustainable transportation and sanitation infrastructure._ | urban infrastructure, transport |
| **rehabilitate** | `verb` | `/ˌriː.həˈbɪl.ɪ.teɪt/` | Phục hồi, cải tạo lại | _The municipality allocated funds to rehabilitate historical town centers._ | rehabilitate buildings, restore |
