| Từ Vựng (Front) | Từ Loại | Phiên Âm (IPA) | Ý Nghĩa Tiếng Việt (Back) | Ví Dụ Ngữ Cảnh Trong Đề Thi | Collocations & Synonyms |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **commute** | `noun/verb` | `/kəˈmjuːt/` | Quãng đường đi làm / Đi làm hàng ngày | _Many respondents face a daily commute of over forty minutes._ | daily commute, commuter train |
| **pedestrian** | `noun` | `/pəˈdes.tri.ən/` | Người đi bộ | _The urban council introduced new safety zones dedicated to pedestrians._ | pedestrian crossing, pedestrian precinct |
| **commitment** | `noun` | `/kəˈmɪt.mənt/` | Sự cam kết, gắn bó trách nhiệm | _Voluntary conservation work requires a minimum six-month commitment._ | make a commitment, demonstrate commitment |
| **eligible** | `adj` | `/ˈel.ɪ.dʒə.bəl/` | Đủ tư cách, đủ điều kiện | _Applicants must be over eighteen to be eligible for the field survey._ | eligible for, eligible to |
| **aeroponics** | `noun` | `/ˌeə.rəʊˈpɒn.ɪks/` | Khí canh (phương pháp trồng cây không cần đất) | _Aeroponics allows crops to grow suspended in air with misted nutrients._ | aeroponics farming, vertical farming |
| **hydroponics** | `noun` | `/ˌhaɪ.drəˈpɒn.ɪks/` | Thủy canh (trồng cây trong dung dịch nước) | _Hydroponics uses mineral nutrient solutions in a water solvent without soil._ | hydroponic system, urban agriculture |
| **yield** | `noun/verb` | `/jiːld/` | Sản lượng / Sinh ra, mang lại | _Vertical agricultural setups produce higher crop yields per square meter._ | high crop yield, agricultural yield |
| **infrastructure** | `noun` | `/ˈɪn.frəˌstrʌk.tʃər/` | Cơ sở hạ tầng | _Urban transport infrastructure must expand to accommodate population growth._ | transport infrastructure, green infrastructure |
| **biodiversity** | `noun` | `/ˌbaɪ.əʊ.daɪˈvɜː.sə.ti/` | Đa dạng sinh học | _Sustainable forest management preserves native biodiversity._ | preserve biodiversity, loss of biodiversity |
| **indigenous** | `adj` | `/ɪnˈdɪdʒ.ɪ.nəs/` | Bản địa, bản xứ | _Local projects reintroduce indigenous tree species to restore degraded soil._ | indigenous species, indigenous community |
| **canopy** | `noun` | `/ˈkæn.ə.pi/` | Tán rừng, vòm che lá cây | _Dense forest canopy filters sunlight and maintains ground humidity._ | forest canopy, under the canopy |
| **ecosystem** | `noun` | `/ˈiː.kəʊˌsɪs.təm/` | Hệ sinh thái | _Uncontrolled logging disrupts the delicate balance of the forest ecosystem._ | fragile ecosystem, marine ecosystem |
| **debris** | `noun` | `/ˈdeb.riː/` | Mảnh vụn, rác thải (vũ trụ/đổ nát) | _Millions of pieces of orbital debris pose a serious threat to active satellites._ | space debris, orbital debris |
| **collision** | `noun` | `/kəˈlɪʒ.ən/` | Sự va chạm, sự đâm nhau | _Tracking systems calculate collision risks between debris and space stations._ | collision course, risk of collision |
| **surveillance** | `noun` | `/səˈveɪ.ləns/` | Sự giám sát chặt chẽ | _Ground-based radar surveillance continuously monitors low-Earth orbit._ | radar surveillance, 24-hour surveillance |
| **orbital** | `adj` | `/ˈɔː.bɪ.təl/` | Thuộc về quỹ đạo | _Spacecraft must navigate safely through dense orbital paths._ | orbital velocity, low Earth orbital |
| **demographic** | `adj/noun` | `/ˌdem.əˈɡræf.ɪk/` | Thuộc nhân khẩu học / Nhóm dân cư | _The graph illustrates demographic shifts across four Asian nations._ | demographic trend, demographic shift |
| **fluctuation** | `noun` | `/ˌflʌk.tʃuˈeɪ.ʃən/` | Sự dao động, biến động | _The percentage showed minor fluctuations before rising steadily from 2000._ | mild fluctuation, market fluctuation |
| **outnumber** | `verb` | `/ˌaʊtˈnʌm.bər/` | Đông hơn, vượt trội về số lượng | _By 2040, urban dwellers are projected to outnumber rural inhabitants._ | outnumber someone, largely outnumber |
| **projection** | `noun` | `/prəˈdʒek.ʃən/` | Dự báo, ước tính tương lai | _According to official projections, the urbanization rate will exceed 70%._ | future projection, demographic projection |
